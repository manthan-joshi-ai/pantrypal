✦ The moment we all know

It's 7pm.
You're staring at the fridge.

The universal dinner dilemma — made worse when your health is in the picture.

You open the fridge and stare at: some chicken, half a bag of spinach, leftover rice, and a lemon. You have no idea what to make — and you're also supposed to be watching your sodium because your doctor said so. So you order pizza. Again.

Every one of us, most nights

This is the gap PantryPal fills. Not a recipe search engine. Not a meal kit. A personal AI chef that knows your ingredients and your health — and bridges them intelligently.

✦ How it works

Three steps to dinner.

Designed to be instant and frictionless — open the app, get cooking.

1

Add your ingredients

Type what's in your fridge and pantry. Include quantities if you want — PantryPal works with whatever you have, however little.

2

Share your health profile

Add chronic conditions (e.g. diabetes, hypertension), dietary restrictions (gluten-free, vegan), or lifestyle goals (high-protein, low-carb). One tap per filter.

3

Get personalized recipes — and chat with your chef

Claude generates recipes tailored to your exact pantry and health. Open Chef Chat on any recipe to ask follow-up questions in real time.

✦ The engine

Why Claude?

This task requires more than text generation. It requires reasoning — across ingredients, health constraints, and culinary creativity simultaneously.

POWERED BY ANTHROPIC CLAUDE
  • ✦
    Intersection reasoning. Claude understands which ingredients pair well and what's safe for someone with Type 2 diabetes and a gluten intolerance — simultaneously, without prompting for each separately.
  • ✦
    Reliable structured output. Claude produces clean, valid JSON that renders directly into rich recipe cards — no post-processing hacks, no retries, no parsing gymnastics.
  • ✦
    Contextual Chef Chat. Each chat session is grounded in the specific recipe — ingredients, instructions, health tags, tips. Claude gives expert, recipe-aware answers rather than generic cooking advice.
  • ✦
    Safety-first generation. Claude is careful not to suggest ingredients that contradict stated health conditions — critical when users are managing real medical needs.
